GLOBAL STYLE RULES (apply to every image unless noted)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Brand: The Animals Among Us
- Tone: calm, documentary, respectful, hopeful | never sensational
- Context: Kenya-first / East Africa everyday life (rural and peri-urban)
- Subjects: domesticated and working animals (donkeys, cattle, oxen, goats,
 sheep, poultry, dogs, cats) and the people who care for them
- DO show: healthy interaction, water, shade, suitable equipment, rest, care
- DO NOT show: graphic injuries, blood, abuse, confrontation, humiliation,
 stereotypes, or staged “rescue drama”
- Prefer natural light; soft morning / golden hour where possible
- People: diverse ages and genders; obtain consent for identifiable faces
- File format preference: JPG (photos) or PNG (logos); web-optimised copies later
- Colour feel: greens, earth tones; avoid purple neon / oversaturated filters

NAMING CONVENTION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Suggested filename pattern:
 tau-[page]-[slot]-[WxH].jpg

Example:
 tau-home-hero-1920x1080.jpg
